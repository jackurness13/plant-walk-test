Plant Walk

U-CREW Energy Assessment Challenge

How to Play

  • 5:00 on the clock. Drag to walk the plant and tap equipment that's wasting energy.
  • Take a reading on each find to build an Assessment Recommendation — its $/yr adds to your score.
  • Watch for decoys: efficient equipment costs you 10 s. A hint shows a zone with a missed find (−20 s).
